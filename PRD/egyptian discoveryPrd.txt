# EGYPTIAN DISCOVERY
## Master Product Requirements Document (PRD)
**Project:** Egyptian Discovery  
**Document type:** Complete Product & Development Specification  
**Platform:** Bilingual Travel Agency Website + E-commerce Store  
**Target market:** Egypt  
**Primary objective:** Build a premium, modern, adventure-focused digital platform for discovering and booking trips across Egypt, alongside an integrated shop for camping equipment and travel gadgets.

---

# 1. PROJECT VISION

## 1.1 Concept

Egyptian Discovery is an Egypt-focused travel platform that combines a professional travel agency website with an integrated e-commerce store.

The platform allows customers to:
- Discover destinations and trips throughout Egypt.
- Explore detailed itineraries, activities, and travel information.
- Register interest in trips and reserve seats.
- Pay deposits and remaining balances.
- Manage personal and group reservations.
- Purchase camping equipment and travel gadgets.
- Earn loyalty points and participate in a monthly member leaderboard.

The platform uses a demand-based reservation model. Customers express interest in destinations and preferred dates, and the agency organizes and confirms trips based on actual paid demand and operational feasibility.

**Important:** This is not a voting website. Customers do not organize trips themselves. The agency controls trip planning, dates, itineraries, pricing, suppliers, and final confirmation.

## 1.2 Main business goals

1. Create a recognizable and professional travel brand.
2. Make discovering and reserving trips easy.
3. Automate repetitive booking and administrative operations.
4. Give customers a clear view of reservation and payment status.
5. Increase customer retention through referrals, loyalty, and rewards.
6. Generate additional revenue through the integrated shop.
7. Give the administrator complete control through one dashboard.

## 1.3 Target audience

Customers located in Egypt who are interested in:
- Adventure and outdoor activities.
- Camping and nature.
- Exploring Egyptian destinations.
- Weekend and multi-day trips.
- Group and family travel.
- Travel equipment and gadgets.

# 5. TRAVEL BOOKING ENGINE

This is one of the most important parts of the entire platform.

The booking engine must follow the business rules below precisely.

## 5.1 Agency-controlled trip creation

The administrator creates every trip.

A trip can include:
- Destination.
- Itinerary.
- Activities.
- Transportation partner.
- Accommodation partner.
- Preferred travel dates.
- Maximum capacity.
- Minimum paid travelers.
- Price per traveler.
- Deposit percentage.
- Booking deadline.
- Departure date.
- Cancellation and refund policy.
- Images and descriptions.

Transportation and accommodation are supplied by external partner companies.

**Partners do not receive accounts or access to the platform.** All partner communication and management happen through the administrator.

## 5.2 Initial reservation process

The customer:

1. Opens a destination or trip.
2. Selects one or more preferred dates.
3. Selects the number of travelers.
4. Submits the reservation.
5. Receives a temporary reservation hold.
6. Uploads payment proof for the required deposit.
7. Waits for administrator verification.

The system must create a separate reservation record and identify the customer, trip, preferred dates, traveler count, and payment status.

### Temporary reservation hold

- Unpaid reservation hold: 12 hours.
- The system automatically expires unpaid holds after 12 hours.
- Expired seats return to available capacity.
- Customers may cancel an initial reservation before paying the deposit.
- Preferred dates cannot be edited after initial registration.
- Multiple destinations may be reserved simultaneously.

### Partial payments

If a customer initially reserves four seats but pays deposits for only two:

- Keep two paid seats.
- Release the other two seats.
- Update the reservation accordingly.
- Only verified paid seats count toward the minimum traveler requirement.

The system must not count unpaid seats as confirmed demand.

## 5.3 Reservation status system

Implement clear reservation statuses, including:

- Pending Payment.
- Payment Under Review.
- Deposit Paid.
- Hold Expired.
- Cancelled.
- Under Review.
- Trip Confirmed.
- Balance Payment Pending.
- Fully Paid.
- Completed.
- Refunded.

Statuses must update according to actual business events.

The customer dashboard and admin dashboard must display consistent statuses.

## 5.4 Demand indicators

Each trip must show:

- Exact paid traveler count.
- Minimum required travelers.
- Progress bar, such as 8/13.
- General status, such as:
  - Gathering Travelers.
  - Almost Confirmed.
  - Under Review.
  - Confirmed.
  - Fully Booked.

These indicators must be based on successfully verified deposits.

## 5.5 Minimum traveler requirement

Default minimum: **13 paid travelers**.

The administrator can change this value for each trip.

Default deadline to reach the minimum: **one month**.

The administrator can configure the deadline for each trip.

When the minimum is reached, the trip enters the Under Review status.

Reaching the minimum must NOT automatically confirm the trip.

## 5.6 Manual trip confirmation

The administrator must manually review the trip before confirming it.

The administrator checks:
- Transportation availability.
- Accommodation availability.
- Supplier availability.
- Operational feasibility.
- Costs.
- Preferred dates selected by customers.

The administrator selects the final trip date and confirms the trip.

### Date compatibility

The system should help the administrator understand which dates are most compatible with paid customer demand.

The final date must respect customers' submitted preferences.

If the administrator selects a date that a customer did not choose:
- Do not automatically move that customer.
- Offer the customer an alternative or refund.
- Record the customer's decision.

## 5.7 If the trip does not reach its minimum

If the trip fails to reach the required minimum before the deadline:

1. The administrator reviews the situation.
2. The trip date may be changed.
3. Existing customers receive the choice to:
   - Accept the new date and keep their reservation.
   - Request a refund.
4. The customer's decision must be stored.
5. Customers who choose a refund are handled through the manual refund process.

The system must not silently change existing reservations.

# 6. TRIP CONFIRMATION AND PRIORITY BOOKING

## 6.1 Priority period

When the administrator confirms a trip:

- A 24-hour priority booking period begins.
- Eligible customers receive priority access.
- The system records the beginning and end of the priority period.

### Eligibility

A customer qualifies if:
- They initially registered for the trip.
- Their reservation is still active.
- Their deposit has been successfully verified.

Customers whose unpaid reservation holds expired are not eligible.

### Additional seat

Each eligible customer may reserve a maximum of one additional seat for a friend or family member.

This additional seat must follow the applicable payment and capacity rules.

Priority access is not a discount.

## 6.2 Public booking

After the 24-hour priority period:
- Remaining seats become available to everyone.
- Public reservations continue until the trip reaches capacity or its booking deadline.
- The system must prevent overselling.

## 6.3 Waiting list

When a trip is full:
- Customers may join a waiting list.
- The waiting list is managed manually by the administrator.
- The administrator can view and manage waiting customers.
- The system must not automatically confirm waiting-list customers.

# 7. PAYMENT SYSTEM

## 7.1 Payment methods

Only the following payment methods are supported at launch:

- InstaPay.
- Bank transfer.

Do not integrate credit card or other online payment gateways unless requested later.

## 7.2 Deposit

Default deposit: **10% of the total trip price**.

The administrator can configure the deposit percentage for each trip.

Customers must upload a screenshot of their payment as proof.

The administrator manually:
- Reviews the uploaded screenshot.
- Checks the payment.
- Approves or rejects the proof.
- Records the verification result.

Only approved payments count as paid.

## 7.3 Payment records

Every payment must have its own record containing:
- Customer.
- Reservation.
- Amount.
- Payment method.
- Uploaded proof.
- Submission date.
- Verification status.
- Administrator who reviewed it.
- Verification date.
- Notes if necessary.

Payment screenshots must be stored securely.

## 7.4 Payment receipts

Generate an automatic receipt after each approved payment.

Receipts should include:
- Egyptian Discovery branding.
- Customer information.
- Payment amount.
- Payment date.
- Related trip or order.
- Payment reference or receipt number.
- Payment status.

Customers must be able to access receipts through their dashboard.

## 7.5 Remaining balance

After trip confirmation:
- The remaining balance becomes due.
- Customers have 48 hours to complete payment.
- Customers upload payment screenshots.
- The administrator manually verifies the payment.

If the customer fails to pay within 48 hours:
- Their seat becomes available for booking again.
- The administrator decides case by case what happens to the original deposit.
- The decision must be recorded.

## 7.6 Refunds

Customers cannot directly request refunds through the website.

They contact the agency through WhatsApp.

The administrator:
- Reviews the request.
- Approves or rejects the refund.
- Records the amount and reason.
- Updates the reservation and payment records.

If the agency cancels a confirmed trip, customers receive a refund.

Customer cancellation and refund rules must be configurable through the admin dashboard.

# 16. SECURITY AND PRIVACY

Security is a critical requirement, particularly because the platform handles payment screenshots and national ID documents.

## 16.1 Authentication

- Secure phone-number-based customer authentication.
- No mandatory customer email.
- Secure administrator login.
- Strong administrator access protection.
- Proper session management.

## 16.2 National ID protection

National ID information is collected only after trip confirmation and the required payment.

Requirements:
- National ID photos must be stored privately.
- Only the main administrator can access national ID photos.
- Customers can submit their own required documents securely.
- Other customers must never access another customer's documents.
- Use secure access controls and protected file URLs.
- Log administrator access where technically appropriate.

## 16.3 Payment security

- Store payment proofs securely.
- Only authorized administrator actions can approve payments.
- Maintain a history of payment decisions.
- Prevent duplicate payment processing.
- Never trust payment status supplied by the client browser.

## 16.4 General security

- Validate all user input on the server.
- Protect customer and admin routes.
- Apply authorization checks to every sensitive operation.
- Use encryption in transit and at rest.
- Maintain database backups.
- Keep audit logs for important administrator actions.
- Implement appropriate data retention and deletion processes.
- Follow applicable Egyptian privacy and data protection requirements.

## 17.2 Data integrity

The database must enforce important business rules.

Examples:
- A reservation cannot exceed available capacity.
- Expired holds cannot count as paid travelers.
- Unverified payments cannot count toward trip minimums.
- A customer cannot receive duplicate rewards for the same qualifying action.
- A shop order cannot be confused with a travel reservation.
- A seat cannot be sold twice.
- Payment records must remain traceable.

Use database transactions and appropriate locking where needed to prevent race conditions and overselling.

# 22. FINAL DEVELOPMENT INSTRUCTIONS

The development agent must follow these instructions throughout the project:

1. **Understand the complete PRD before implementing features.** Do not start by building disconnected pages.

2. **Prioritize functionality and design equally.** The website must look exceptional and operate reliably.

3. **Build a real working platform.** Do not create buttons that are only decorative or forms that do not save data.

4. **Use a proper database.** Do not rely on local storage or hardcoded sample data as the actual production system.

5. **Keep business logic on the server.** Payment verification, reservation expiration, seat availability, rewards, and access control must not rely on client-side calculations alone.

6. **Build a complete admin dashboard.** The administrator must be able to manage the business without changing code.

7. **Respect every business rule.** Especially the reservation hold, partial payments, paid traveler count, manual trip confirmation, priority period, payment deadlines, and national ID collection conditions.

8. **Do not collect unnecessary customer information.** Registration requires only a phone number. Email and national ID are not registration requirements.

9. **Protect sensitive documents.** National ID photos are accessible only to the main administrator.

10. **Make all specified business settings configurable.** Do not hardcode values that the administrator should control.

11. **Support Arabic and English properly.** RTL and LTR must be implemented throughout the platform, not just translated text.

12. **Use realistic, high-quality imagery.** Destination and product imagery should be relevant, attractive, and consistent with the brand.

13. **Do not invent business features.** The blog is not part of the launch scope, and partners do not need accounts.

14. **Test complete workflows.** A feature is not finished until its customer experience, administrator controls, database behavior, and edge cases have been tested.

15. **Do not finalize hosting prematurely.** Hosting and production deployment are decisions for the final phase after development is complete.

16. **Keep the code maintainable.** Use reusable components, clear naming, proper documentation, and a scalable architecture.

17. **Do not sacrifice reliability for visual effects.** Animations and visual polish must never interfere with booking, payments, or accessibility.

18. **Report progress clearly.** Maintain a list of completed features, remaining tasks, known issues, and decisions requiring clarification.
